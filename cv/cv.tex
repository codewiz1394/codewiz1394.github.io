\cvsection{Objective}
\justifying
I work at the crossroads of robotics, safety, and intelligent
decision-making, building agents that can learn, adapt, and act reliably
even when faced with adversarial inputs. From training DRL agents in
simulation to testing them on real vehicles equipped with LiDAR, GNSS, and
camera systems, I aim to bridge the gap between research and reality in
embodied AI.\par
